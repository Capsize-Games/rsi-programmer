Dataset: Accelerometer (UCI Machine Learning Repository, dataset 846)
Official record: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/846/accelerometer
Official archive:
https://archive.ics.uci.edu/static/public/846/accelerometer.zip
Citation: Scalabrini Sampaio, G., Rabello de Aguiar Vallim Filho, A.,
Santos de Silva, L., & Augusto da Silva, L. (2019). Accelerometer [Dataset].
UCI Machine Learning Repository. https://doi.org/10.24432/C5Q61V.
Related paper: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6806350/.
The paper describes the physical fan rig and extrapolates failure time from
the measured data; this project does not use its failure-time labels.
License stated by UCI: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
(CC BY 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.

Reproducible official download command (run from the repository root):
curl --fail --location --silent --show-error \
  https://archive.ics.uci.edu/static/public/846/accelerometer.zip \
  --output data/sensing_fan_v1/accelerometer.zip

The official UCI record describes vibration recordings from one cooler fan
with three blade-weight configurations and 17 fan-speed settings. It states
that measurements were collected every 20 ms for one minute per setting.
The CSV itself has no timestamp column. Treat 50 Hz as the nominal rate from
the UCI description, not as timestamps measured in the file.
